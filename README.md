<h2>🚗 Android Auto Setup</h2>

<p>If Escapify does not immediately appear in Android Auto:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Open <strong>Android Auto</strong> on your device.</li>
  <li>Navigate to <strong>Settings</strong> ➔ scroll to the bottom and tap the <strong>Version number</strong> 10 times to enable Developer Settings.</li>
  <li>Tap the <strong>three dots menu</strong> (⋮) in the top-right corner ➔ select <strong>Developer settings</strong>.</li>
  <li>Check and enable <strong>Unknown sources</strong>.</li>
  <li>Restart Android Auto and connect to your vehicle.</li>
</ol>
